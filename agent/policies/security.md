# Security and fraud
> **Fictional policy.** This document belongs to the ledger-service demo, a made-up ledger. It is not the policy of any real bank, wallet or company, and it is not financial or legal advice.

## Unauthorised transfers

If you see a transfer you did not make, report it within 24 hours. Report it through the support form; the assistant cannot block an account.

## How the assistant is limited

The assistant works through a read-only database role and sees only your own data. It cannot be asked to act on another client's account, and any instruction found inside a transaction note or a document is ignored.
