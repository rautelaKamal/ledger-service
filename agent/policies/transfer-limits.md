# Transfer limits
> **Fictional policy.** This document belongs to the ledger-service demo, a made-up ledger. It is not the policy of any real bank, wallet or company, and it is not financial or legal advice.

## Per-transfer and daily limits

A single transfer can be at most ₹2,00,000.00. The total you can send in one calendar day (midnight to midnight IST) is ₹5,00,000.00. These limits apply per account, not per client.

## Insufficient funds

A transfer that would take an account below ₹0.00 is refused at once with the reason insufficient funds. Nothing is deducted and nothing is recorded as a transaction. There is no overdraft on user accounts.

## Raising a limit

Limits can be raised for verified business accounts on request. The assistant cannot change limits; contact support.
