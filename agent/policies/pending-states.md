# Pending, posted and failed
> **Fictional policy.** This document belongs to the ledger-service demo, a made-up ledger. It is not the policy of any real bank, wallet or company, and it is not financial or legal advice.

## The three states

Every transaction is pending, posted or failed. Pending means recorded but not yet final: no money has moved. Posted means final: the entries are written and balances are updated. Failed means it will never post.

## Transfers

A transfer is posted the moment it is accepted; it is never pending. If you see a transfer, the money has moved.

## Top-ups

A top-up starts pending and becomes posted when the provider confirms it, or failed when the provider reports a failure. A pending top-up is not part of your balance.
