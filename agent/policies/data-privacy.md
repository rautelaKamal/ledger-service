# Data and privacy
> **Fictional policy.** This document belongs to the ledger-service demo, a made-up ledger. It is not the policy of any real bank, wallet or company, and it is not financial or legal advice.

## What the assistant can see

The assistant can read only the accounts you own and the transactions those accounts took part in. It cannot see other clients' accounts, names or balances, and it cannot see the stored responses of your API requests or the raw messages from the payment provider.

## What the assistant cannot do

The assistant cannot move money, change limits, open disputes or close accounts. It has no write access to the ledger. Every question you ask is recorded, with the data the assistant looked up, for 90 days.

## Third parties

Questions are processed by an external AI model provider. In this demo all data is fictional. A real deployment would process customer data only under a contract that forbids the provider from using it to train models.
