# Failed top-ups and refunds
> **Fictional policy.** This document belongs to the ledger-service demo, a made-up ledger. It is not the policy of any real bank, wallet or company, and it is not financial or legal advice.

## What a failed top-up means

A top-up fails when the payment provider reports that the payment did not succeed. A failed top-up never credits your account: no money is added and no entry is written to the ledger.

## How long a refund takes

If money left your bank account or card for a top-up that then failed, the provider returns it. Refunds to UPI and bank accounts take 1 to 3 business days; refunds to cards take 5 to 7 business days, depending on the card issuer. The ledger does not hold the money at any point during a refund.

## What to do if a refund is late

If a refund has not arrived 7 business days after the failure, contact support with the top-up id. Support can see the provider's payment reference and chase it. Refunds are never paid out of your ledger balance.
