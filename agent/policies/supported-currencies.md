# Currencies
> **Fictional policy.** This document belongs to the ledger-service demo, a made-up ledger. It is not the policy of any real bank, wallet or company, and it is not financial or legal advice.

## Supported currencies

Accounts are held in a single currency, shown as a three-letter code. Top-ups are available for INR accounts only. A top-up in another currency is refused at creation.

## No conversion

A transfer between accounts of different currencies is refused. The ledger never converts money.
