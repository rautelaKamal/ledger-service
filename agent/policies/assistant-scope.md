# What the assistant can answer
> **Fictional policy.** This document belongs to the ledger-service demo, a made-up ledger. It is not the policy of any real bank, wallet or company, and it is not financial or legal advice.

## In scope

Questions about your own balances, your own transactions and top-ups, and about these policies. Examples: why a balance changed, whether a top-up went through, how long a refund takes.

## Out of scope

Anything else: other people's accounts, moving money, legal or tax advice, general knowledge. The assistant says so and points you to support where relevant.

## Verification

Every figure the assistant states is checked against the data it actually looked up. If a figure cannot be checked, the assistant says it could not verify its answer rather than guessing.
