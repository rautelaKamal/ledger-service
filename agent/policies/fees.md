# Fees
> **Fictional policy.** This document belongs to the ledger-service demo, a made-up ledger. It is not the policy of any real bank, wallet or company, and it is not financial or legal advice.

## Transfers

Transfers between accounts in the ledger are free of charge. There is no fee for sending or receiving.

## Top-ups

Top-ups by UPI and bank transfer are free. Top-ups by credit card carry a fee of 2% of the amount, charged by the payment provider and shown as a separate line on the provider's receipt, not on your ledger statement.

## Other fees

There are no account maintenance fees, no fees for statements and no fees for closing an account.
