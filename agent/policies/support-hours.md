## Hours

Human support is available Monday to Saturday, 09:00 to 18:00 IST, excluding national holidays. The assistant is available at all times.

## What to have ready

For questions about a specific transaction, keep its transaction id ready; the assistant shows it with every explanation. For a top-up, the top-up id is the transaction id.
