# Disputes
> **Fictional policy.** This document belongs to the ledger-service demo, a made-up ledger. It is not the policy of any real bank, wallet or company, and it is not financial or legal advice.

## What can be disputed

You can dispute a transfer you did not authorise, a top-up that was confirmed for the wrong amount, or a transaction you believe was recorded twice. Disputes must be raised within 60 days of the transaction date.

## How a dispute works

Support freezes nothing: the ledger is append-only, so a disputed transaction is never edited or deleted. If the dispute is upheld, a reversing transaction is posted, which appears as a separate entry on your statement.

## Timelines

You receive a first response within 2 business days and a decision within 15 business days. The assistant can explain a transaction but cannot open a dispute; use the support form.
