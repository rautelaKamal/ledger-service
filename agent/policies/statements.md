## What a statement shows

A statement lists every posted transaction on an account in a period, with the amount in rupees and paise, the direction (money in or money out), the other account's id, and the balance after each transaction.

## Periods

Statements are available for any period up to 12 months. Balances on a statement are closing balances at the end of the last day of the period, India time.

## Pending items

Pending top-ups are listed separately at the end of a statement and are not included in the closing balance.
