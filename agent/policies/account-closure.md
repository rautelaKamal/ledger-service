# Closing an account
> **Fictional policy.** This document belongs to the ledger-service demo, a made-up ledger. It is not the policy of any real bank, wallet or company, and it is not financial or legal advice.

## Conditions

An account can be closed when its balance is exactly ₹0.00 and it has no pending top-ups. Transfer the remaining balance to another account first.

## What happens to the history

The ledger keeps every transaction of a closed account. Statements remain available for 7 years after closure, as required by the demo's fictional record-keeping rules.

## Reopening

A closed account cannot be reopened. Open a new account instead; its history starts empty.
